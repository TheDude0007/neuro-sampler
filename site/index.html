Neuro Sampler Suite · v1.13 · VST3 / AU / Standalone

A sampler that listens.

An MPC-style slice sampler that hears the key, pitch, chords and drums of everything you load, then puts that knowledge to work: in-key chord pads, a theory brain that suggests what comes next, melodic arpeggios, an evolving sequencer and one-sample-accurate chopping. Open source.

Why it stands apart

Most samplers play what you load. This one understands it.

Six ideas run through the whole instrument. Each one is measured in the test suite, and most of the tests check rendered audio.

Listening

It hears the sample

Pitch per slice (YIN, with confidence), key per sample and per slice, chord names with inversions, and drum class per hit. A key written in the file name wins, and the closest other keys are offered.

Theory

Harmony is built in

Chord pads, Harmonize, and Timbre chords that build each chord from several slices. A theory brain learned from real progressions suggests the next chord and writes progressions into the sequencer.

Generative

Controlled randomness

Mutation, Phrase Memory, Tonnetz walks and Euclid rhythms are all seeded. The same seed always gives the same result, and you get A/B and 64 levels of undo.

Precision

One-sample chops

Jog wheels on the MPK knobs move slice edges 1 sample per click. Note Repeat goes down to 1/128 with sample-exact timing, and MIDI clock follows to the sample.

AI, on your machine

Stems and kits

Demucs splits a full song into Drums, Bass, Vocals and Other, then a second pass adds Lows and Mids. NMF pulls Kick, Snare and Cymbals out of a mixed loop, and you can teach it your kit.

Engineering

Built to hold up

Lock-free audio thread, about 340 automated checks, pluginval strictness 10, and about 0.4% of one core at the worst case measured. All 234 controls can be MIDI-mapped, and a test enforces it.

CHORD tab · v1.9 + theory brain v1.12

Every pad knows the key.

Below is the same harmony logic the plugin uses, running in your browser. Choose a degree, a shape and a voicing to see and hear the chord.

Module 01

Chord lab

The CHORD tab has three ways to make chords from your slices:

HARMONIZEEvery pitched slice plays the song key's chord built on its own note. Drum slices stay single.
CHORD PADSPads 1–8 of a bank are the key's chords I–VII plus the octave. Pads 9–16 play the same chords in one richer shape.
TIMBRE CHORDSEach chord tone comes from a different slice, so a chord can be a vocal, a bass and a pluck at once.
RENDERKeep length stretches each note to the original duration. Resample pitches it like a tape. Strum spreads the notes over time.

Module 02

Theory brain

A Markov model of chord changes in scale degrees. It is trained on 110 real progressions (the MIT free-midi-chords set) plus your own chord library when it finds one, which never leaves your machine. A functional-harmony prior (V→I, ii→V, IV→V) keeps rare moves sensible.

Next: four buttons suggest the most likely next chords after the one you just played. Generate: builds a 4- or 8-chord progression. With a mood selected, it picks a real progression tagged with that mood. With no mood, it takes a smart walk from the tonic that ends on a chord leading home. → SEQ writes the progression into the sequencer. Chord MIDI out sends the chords to a synth.

Moods: Dark · Dramatic · Empowered · Excited · Hopeful · Joyful · Lonely · Mysterious · Nostalgic · Peaceful · Playful · Rebellious · Relaxed · Romantic · Sad · Spiritual · Tender · Triumphant

Arpeggiator · 14 directions

Ten classic directions, four melodic ones.

The held chord is Cm7, played in the order E♭, C, B♭, G, with the scale set to C minor. Pick a direction to see its notes on the piano roll.

Module 03

Arp visualiser

On top of the direction: 1/1–1/32 with triplets, gate, 1–4 octaves, MPC swing, probability, repeats, latch, retrigger. The GEN tab adds a 16-step rhythm with accents and ratchets, Euclid fill, Arpeggio Grammar (up to 8 rules you can read and edit, such as "step 4 of every 8: octave jump"), Phrase Memory (records a phrase and replays it with seeded variation, returning to the original every C cycles), humanise and a gesture XY pad.

Keys mode · Play In · scale snapping

Thirteen scales, one wheel.

Keys mode plays a slice chromatically from its detected root and snaps notes to the scale. Play In moves every musical slice to another key and keeps their length. Click a note on the wheel to hear it.

Module 04

Scale wheel

Step sequencer · 16 patterns × 16 lanes

Patterns that evolve, with nothing lost.

Each lane can have its own length (polymeter), and each step its own velocity, gate, probability, ratchet, microtiming and pitch. Trig conditions decide when a step plays. Click a step to toggle it, then Mutate.

Module 05

Pattern Genome

TRIG CONDITIONSAlways · A:B (cycle A of every B) · First / not first · Fill / not fill · If previous / if not previous.
EVOLVEReverse, shift, ×2, ÷2, expand, contract, velocity curves, probability profiles. Each change is one undo step.
GENOMEA lane becomes genes: rhythm, velocity, pitch, groove, chance, ratchet and gate. Take the groove from pattern 3 and the pitches from pattern 7.
MUTATIONTicked genes mutate and the rest stay exactly as they were. Set intensity, the % of steps to preserve, a density bias and a seed, then compare A/B.
WEAVEAccents, fills and pitch can be borrowed from other patterns on top of the playing one.
CHOP tab · jog chop v1.11 · Note Repeat

Chop down to the single sample.

Four chop modes (Transients, Grid 4–128, Kit, Manual), magnetic snap to hits, a crosshair, keyboard nudges and 16 Levels. Jog chop maps to the MPK mini's K5 and K6 knobs. Drag the knobs below up or down.

Module 06

Jog chop

START moves the selected slice's start. END moves its end and the next slice's start with it. On the first slice, START cuts the head off into a slice of its own. On the last slice, END cuts off the tail.

Step size is 1 sample, 0.1 ms, 1 ms or 10 ms per click, or follow zoom. While you turn, the waveform zooms to the moving edge. When the knob stops, the slice auditions after 180 ms.

Module 07

Note Repeat XY

Hold REPEAT and play pads to retrigger them at the rate. The rates run from 1 bar down to 1/128, with triplets. Gate is 1–100%, and a short gate on any sample gives stutter and glitch chops. Swing runs 50–75%, with Latch and Lock.

On the Repeat XY pad, left to right sets the rate and bottom to top sets the gate. Slide while you play for trap rolls that speed up and for gate sweeps. Hits lock to the host's bar grid, so 1/16 at 120 BPM lands at exactly 0, 6000, 12000 and 18000 samples.

Key detection · measured

Honest ears.

Key detection on stems and loops is hard, and most of the misses are a fifth or a fourth away from the right key. So the sampler shows how sure it is, offers the closest other keys, and uses the key from the file name when there is one.

Benchmarked on 713 labelled loops. "Exact" means the right key. "Same notes" also counts the relative key (C major vs A minor), which uses the same seven notes.

A v1.11 HPCP prototype added spectral peaks, tuning estimation and harmonic voting. It gained a little and is documented in the repo. The change that solved real projects was letting the file name win: 11 of 11 labelled loops got the right key on load.

The key menu offers "Use key from file name" plus the runner-up keys, so you can fix any miss in one click.
